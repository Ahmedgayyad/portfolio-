EquiCare

Period
Dec 2025 – Feb 2026
Type
Horse Services Mobile App
Services
Mobile UI/UX, Design System

About

Designed a complete mobile experience for horse owners to discover services, shop equestrian products, and manage bookings. The focus was on creating a clean, user-friendly interface that simplifies navigation and provides a seamless experience across the entire app.

Outcome

The final product delivers a complete equestrian mobile ecosystem: horse owners can browse certified veterinarians, farriers, trainers, transport services, and shop equestrian gear with a unified checkout flow.

The mobile interface prioritizes quick access with clear visual hierarchy, making complex booking workflows simple and intuitive on smaller screens.

Problem

Horse owners struggle with fragmented communication across different providers for equine health, training, transport, and equipment purchasing.

Booking specialized services like equine transport or farrier visits often requires back-and-forth phone calls without transparent pricing or schedule confirmation.

The challenge was designing a mobile application that consolidates service discovery, product shopping, and booking management into one cohesive and accessible mobile interface.

Research

I conducted user interviews with horse owners, stable managers, and equestrian service providers to map out key mobile journeys and pain points.

Key research insights included:

  • Users need fast emergency access to equine vet services.
  • Clear service pricing and rating reviews build trust before booking.
  • Mobile navigation must be thumb-friendly for quick one-handed use outdoors at stables.

Product System

To keep the app scalable, I designed an equestrian UI design system with reusable mobile components, unified tab bars, bottom sheets, and consistent card layouts.

Role

I led the end-to-end product design, covering user research, wireframing, mobile UI design, interactive prototyping, and developer handoff.

Learnings

1. Mobile UX is about context.

Designing for mobile means considering outdoor usability, quick gestures, and clear contrast for high visibility in different environments.

2. Streamlined checkout increases conversion.

Combining service booking with instant product checkout reduced friction and improved user retention.
